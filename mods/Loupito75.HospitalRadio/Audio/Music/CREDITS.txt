Notes

Attribution-required tracks should retain, where available, the original track
title, artist name, license, and source information.

NonCommercial licenses (CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-ND) permit noncommercial use only.

NoDerivatives licenses prohibit sharing adapted versions. Hospital Radio's OGG
conversion is intended only as a technical format conversion and must not apply
creative edits to CC BY-NC-ND tracks.

Hospital Radio playback model
-----------------------------
Hospital Radio distributes music files as separate audio assets. The tracks are
not edited, remixed, or embedded into a pre-rendered audiovisual work.

During gameplay, tracks are selected and played dynamically as an independent
radio stream. Playback is not intentionally timed, edited, or synchronized to a
specific scene, animation, cinematic sequence, character action, or visual
event. The music may simply be heard while gameplay continues independently.
